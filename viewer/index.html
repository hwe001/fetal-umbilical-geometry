Fetal Umbilical Geometry

Real digitized cord sheath & placenta, the umbilical vein (UV) centreline recovered from the original CMISS 1D source mesh, and a synthetic umbilical artery (UA) pair generated from it. Companion data/code for the manuscript "A Gestational-Age-Continuous Digital Twin of the Fetal Umbilical Circulation."
